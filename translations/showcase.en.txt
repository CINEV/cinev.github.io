Frequently asked questions\nabout animation production
Can I start with just a few original images?
Yes. Start with materials you already have, such as character designs, original artwork, webtoon panels, or picture-book pages. First use CINEV to organize the scene you want to create and any additional materials it needs.
Can I try one scene rather than a full webtoon episode?
Yes. Begin with a pilot of a key scene to assess animation potential and character movement, then expand it into an episode.
How closely can the original artwork and characters be preserved?
Generate and compare scene candidates based on the original images and character references, then choose the result that fits the work. The original composition, colors, and visual impression serve as production standards.
Can a pilot expand into episodes or a webtoon promotional video?
Yes. Once the characters, scenes, and directing approach are established, continue with follow-up scenes, episodes, teasers, and webtoon promotional videos.
Can I commission production if I cannot make it myself?
Yes. CINEV Studio can review the original work and your goals, then collaborate on the required scope, from concept and storyboard to animation and editing.
How are rights and permitted uses of the original and final work determined?
Start with original works and assets you have the right to use. For supported production projects, public presentation and usage rights for the results are reviewed and agreed upon individually before contracting.
