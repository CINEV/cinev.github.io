The technology behind
our stories and worlds.
CineV's vision went beyond generating individual video clips: we sought a production experience for creating a complete story and the world in which it lives.
Starting with data captured in our own motion capture studio and research into AI motion, we developed 3D video production tools built on Unreal Engine. We then combined AI image and video generation with precise directing tools, connecting story development with storyboarding and video production.
Fast generation and fine control. Stories and worlds that continue beyond individual scenes. This archive records CineV's journey toward that vision and the technology we built along the way.

A 3D studio for your characters
and your creative direction
We built an Unreal Engine-based streaming studio so users could view and edit real 3D backgrounds, characters, and motion assets directly in a web browser. Character placement, actions, camera composition, and the timeline could be adjusted to create scenes that matched their creative intent.

CineV Studio: Build scenes with your characters
Place VRM and ZEPETO characters in a scene and set their actions and camera targets. Carry your 3D edits into the next production stage. Additional characters, outfits, appearance options, and background maps expanded the range of people and places, while natural idle motion during LookAt refined dialogue scenes.
CineV Studio: Generate AI actions
Enter a prompt to generate the action you need. Text-to-Motion support for male characters and action trimming help tailor movement to the scene.
CineV Studio: Edit action positions and timing
Select multiple actions and move their starting positions together. Move clips, add or remove empty intervals, reorder character tracks, and use undo and redo to fine-tune the pacing of a scene.
CineV Studio: Direct the camera composition
Use an expanded set of camera templates, VRM targeting, and framing based on the center of multiple characters. Selected targets are preserved when changing templates, making it easier to try different compositions.
